Who we are

Holy Marmot is a game studio based in Istanbul, Turkey. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the "data controller").

For any question about this policy or your data, including privacy requests, email us at contact@holymarmot.com.

Game

Rocket Operator 3D

iOS Android

This section covers Rocket Operator 3D on iOS and Android. When something applies to only one platform, we say so.

What the game doesn't collect

The game doesn't ask you to create an account. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

Services and data

The game uses the services below. For each one, we list what data it receives and why.

GameAnalytics

Analytics

We use GameAnalytics for analytics, to understand how the game is played.

Data it receives
  • In-game events: level starts and finishes, play time, in-game currency transactions and ad events
  • Device and operating system information
  • Session information
  • A GameAnalytics user ID
  • On iOS: the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • On Android: the advertising ID

Privacy policy: GameAnalytics Privacy Notice

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads

Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads. They use the data below to show ads and to measure them.

Data they receive
  • Advertising ID: the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID (GAID) on Android
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Ad impression and click data

These providers process this data under their own privacy policies.

Privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (covers Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource)

Our analytics server (api.holymarmot.com)

Analytics

The game sends a copy of the events it sends to GameAnalytics to our own server, api.holymarmot.com, together with:

  • a randomly generated installation ID
  • a session ID
  • the app version
  • the platform (iOS or Android)

We don't link this data to a person or an account. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

In-app purchases (App Store and Google Play)

Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

The game keeps only a summary of each transaction on your device, so that the same purchase isn't given to you twice.

Privacy policies: App Store & Privacy (Apple), Google Payments Privacy Notice

Game Center and Google Play Games

Leaderboards

The game uses your Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) player ID and display name for leaderboards: trophies, coins and FreeFall score. Other players can see your display name and your scores.

Privacy policies: Game Center & Privacy (Apple), Google Privacy Policy

iCloud and Google Play Saved Games

Cloud saves

Your game progress is backed up and synced across your devices with iCloud (iOS) or Google Play Saved Games (Android). This data is stored in your own Apple or Google account.

Privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy

Notifications

On device

The game uses local notifications to let you know when your energy has refilled or your daily reward is ready. They are scheduled on your device. We don't use remote push notifications.

Your choices

All of these are done through your device rather than inside the game.

  • Tracking (iOS). The game can only access your IDFA if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads. The exact path depends on your device and Android version.
  • Notifications. You can turn the game's notifications on or off in your device's system settings.
  • Deleting the game. Deleting the game removes the data stored on your device, including the installation ID. It doesn't remove data that has already been sent to our server or to the services above, or progress saved in your iCloud or Google Play account.

How long we keep data

We keep event data on our analytics server (api.holymarmot.com) for 24 months after it's collected, then delete it. We may keep totals, such as daily player counts, that can't be linked to an installation ID. If we stop supporting the game, we delete the remaining data within 90 days.

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the game. Unity, Apple and Google keep the data they handle according to their own policies.

Asking about or deleting your data

You can email contact@holymarmot.com to ask what data we hold about you or to ask us to delete it.

To be upfront about what we can do: the game has no accounts, and the installation ID is random and isn't shown to you anywhere in the game. The data on our server and in GameAnalytics is linked to identifiers like the installation ID and device IDs, not to your name or email address. This means we usually can't tell which records are yours from an email alone. If we can't find your data, we'll tell you so in our reply.

Whatever we can or can't find, you can always:

  • delete the data on your device by deleting the game;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID (see Your choices);
  • contact Unity, Apple or Google directly about the data they handle, using the policy links above.

Children

Rocket Operator 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you have concerns about this, contact us at contact@holymarmot.com.

Your rights under GDPR and KVKK

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (KVKK) give you rights over your personal data. These include the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it deleted;
  • object to how we process it.

To use any of these rights, email us at contact@holymarmot.com. Each game's section explains what we can and can't find. For Rocket Operator 3D, see Asking about or deleting your data.

International transfers

The third-party services named in this policy may process your data in countries other than the one you live in, including countries outside Turkey and the European Economic Area.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll publish the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
